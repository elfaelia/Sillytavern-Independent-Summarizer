Independent Summarizer

Keeps one evolving story summary using a separate Connection Profile. Your roleplay preset, character card, World Info, Author's Note, and IHYLL instructions are not sent to the summarizer.

Choose the Connection Profile containing the model that should write summaries, such as your OpenRouter profile. This does not change your roleplay model.

This counts both your messages and the character's messages. Older waiting messages are handled first so the story stays in order.
